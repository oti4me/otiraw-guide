OtiRAW User Guide

Assist: help with culling

Assist looks at every photo and tells you what it found: which frames are sharp, where eyes are closed, which photos are the same shot, and which of each group is best. It only ever suggests. Nothing is marked or deleted until you say so.

Everything is measured on your computer. No photo leaves it.

Faces, eyes and similar-photo groups need Assist’s models. Download them in Settings › Models. Without them Assist still measures sharpness, motion and exposure.

What Assist measures

MeasureWhat it means
SharpnessHow sharp the photo is, overall and where the focus fell
SteadinessWhether there is motion blur
ExposureWhether the photo, or the faces in it, are too dark or too bright
FacesHow many faces, and whether one is cut off by the frame
Eyes, Eyes OpenHow sharp each face’s eyes are, and whether they are open
SmileWhether faces are smiling
SimilarityWhich photos are the same shot (a burst) or the same scene (another pose or framing)

Photos are analysed in the background as a folder’s previews are built, about a tenth of a second each. To analyse now, select photos and choose Photo › Analyse Photos (⌥⇧⌘U).

Groups and suggestions

Assist gathers photos into groups: frames of the same shot, and shots of the same scene. Within a group it judges each photo against the others, so “soft” means softer than the group’s sharpest, and “eyes closed” means closed here while open in the other frames.

Each photo then gets a suggestion with its reasons:

  • a suggested pick: the best of its group;
  • a suggested reject: with a reason such as eyes closed, soft, motion blur, a face cut off, or faces much darker or brighter than the group’s;
  • to review: a close call Assist won’t decide.

The Assist panel

The Assist panel shows the active photo’s suggestion, its reasons and its numbers, and:

  • Eyes at 100 %: each face’s eyes, cropped at full size, so you can check focus without zooming.
  • Its group, best first: the other frames of the group as thumbnails.
  • Compare Group (⇧C) and Survey Group (⇧N): open the group in Compare or Survey.

Move between groups with ⌥→ and ⌥←.

Tune

Open Tune in the panel to set how Assist judges.

ControlWhat it does
StrictnessCautious at 0, Balanced at 50, Ruthless at 100
Each CheckEach check’s own level (eye and frame sharpness, motion blur, closed eyes, exposure, cut-off faces). Each follows the strictness until you move it; ↺ puts it back
Keep of a GroupHow many of each group are suggested as picks
Grouping › Same Shot, Same SceneHow alike photos must be to count as the same shot or scene
Overlay and BadgesHow strongly the loupe’s face outlines and the grid’s badges are drawn, and Dim the Rest

Seeing it in the grid and loupe

  • Grid badges mark photos with eyes closed, soft focus, motion blur, a cut-off face or bad exposure, and a corner mark shows the suggestion. Each kind of badge is off until you turn it on under Grid badges in the panel.
  • View › Show/Hide Assist Overlay (⌥A) outlines each face on the loupe, coloured by how sharp its eyes are.
  • View › Show/Hide Soft Photo Badges (⌥⌘S) marks soft photos in the grid.

Filter and sort by what Assist found

The filter bar’s Assist menu shows photos by: Eyes Closed, Soft, Motion Blur, Face Cut Off, With Faces or Without Faces, Best in Group or Not Best, groups of at least a number, and Exact Copies. Clear Assist Criteria removes them.

Sort by Assist Group to keep each group together, or Assist Focus for sharpest first. With Stack by: Similarity on, each group is a stack with its best photo on top (see Stacks).

Assisted Cull

Photo › Assisted Cull… (⌥⌘U) turns the suggestions into marks, all at once.

SectionChoices
PhotosAll in the folder, or only the selected ones
KeepThe best of each group (how many), or a share of the photos. At least one of every group is always kept
MarkPicks as the Pick flag (or a rating or label you choose); rejects as the Reject flag; close calls with a review label
Change photos already marked tooOff: photos you have marked yourself are left alone

Before applying, the dialog counts the suggested picks, rejects and photos to review, so you see what will happen. Click Apply.

  • It is one undo step: ⌘Z takes it all back.
  • Nothing is deleted. Rejects are only flagged. Removing them is a separate command (Delete Rejected Photos…), which asks.

Learning from your picks

With Learn from my picks (on this computer) on, Assist compares its suggestions with what you actually pick and adjusts, separately for each kind of shoot (wedding, portrait…). It needs a number of your picks before the learning counts, and says how many it has. Forget What I’ve Picked clears it.

Assisted Review

The Assisted Review panel is a simpler view of the same checks: three questions asked of every photo, and the photos counted by the answers.

What is checked

ControlWhat it does
Check: Closed Eyes, Soft Eyes, Poor ExposureWhich of the three are looked for
Photos: Show TagsA small tag on each photo in the grid and filmstrip for what was found. Its edge is amber for likely, red for found, grey for unsure
Review as Folders OpenReviews every folder automatically. Off, photos are reviewed only when you ask
Review SelectedReviews the selected photos now

Results

The results are a small table. Each row is a check; each column says how sure Assist is:

ColumnMeans
LikelyProbably so: worth a look
FoundFound, surely
UnsureCouldn’t be told (no face found, eyes hidden)

Under them, No Issues counts the photos with nothing found, and Not Reviewed those not looked at yet.

Every count is a filter. Click a count to show only those photos; click it again to stop. Several counts can be on at once. Click a column’s name to turn the whole column on or off.

At the foot of the panel:

  • Clear Filters shows every photo again.
  • Reset Review (⇧⌘U) drops the selected photos’ review so they can be reviewed again.
