OtiRAW User Guide

Print

The Print workspace lays photos out on pages and prints them, or saves the pages as files for a lab. Press ⌘P, or click Print in the workspace switcher. ⌘P again goes back to the Library.

The layout

PartWhat it holds
LeftA Preview of the page, the Template Browser (OtiRAW’s templates and yours) and Saved Prints
CentreThe page as it will print, with rulers and guides
Bar under the pageThe page arrows and Use: which photos. Page Setup… and Print Settings… are at the foot of the left panels
RightThe layout and print settings
FilmstripThe folder’s photos

Which photos

Use: chooses what is laid out: Selected Photos, All Filmstrip Photos or Flagged Photos.

Paper

Page Setup… opens the system’s dialog for the paper size and orientation. Paper in the right panel shows the choice, with Portrait and Landscape.

Templates give a quick start: Maximise Size, Fine Art Mat, With a White Border, 2 × 2 Cells, and contact sheets. Save this layout as a template keeps yours under User Templates.

Picture Package and Custom Package

  • Add to Package adds a cell of a standard size, in inches, to the page shown if there is room.
  • Drag a cell to place it; drag its handles to size it. Adjust Selected Cell sets its size exactly. Lock to Photo Aspect Ratio makes each cell take its photo’s shape.
  • In a Custom Package, drag a photo from the filmstrip into a cell.
  • Auto Layout packs every cell again from the first page. Clear Layout takes every cell off the page.
  • New Page adds an empty page; Delete Page removes one and its cells.

Color Management

This is what decides whether the print matches the screen. Choose one of two ways and make sure the other is off.

WaySet hereSet in the printer’s dialog
OtiRAW manages colour (recommended)Profile: the ICC profile for your printer and paper. Intent: Perceptual or Relative. Black Point Compensation onTurn the printer’s own colour management off
Managed by PrinterProfile: Managed by PrinterTurn the printer’s colour management on

Printing with both on, or both off, is the usual cause of prints with a colour cast.

Paper profiles come with the printer’s driver or from the paper’s maker, and are installed in the system. Other Profile… chooses one from a file.

SettingWhat it does
Intent: PerceptualMoves every colour in a little to keep their relations
Intent: RelativeKeeps the colours the paper can hold exactly and clips the rest
Soft ProofShows the page as it will print on this paper
Simulate Paper & InkShows the paper’s own white and the ink’s black, not the screen’s
Print AdjustmentBrightness and Contrast for the print only, for prints that come out darker or flatter than the screen

Saved prints

Create Saved Print keeps this layout and print job with these photos, under Saved Prints. Open it later to print the same thing again.

If some of a saved print’s photos aren’t in the filmstrip, a message says how many: open their folder to print them.

Printing darker than the screen?

Screens are usually far brighter than paper in a room. In order:

  1. Lower your screen’s brightness to a level meant for print work.
  2. Check that only one of OtiRAW and the printer is managing colour.
  3. Use the paper’s own profile, not a generic one.
  4. For what remains, use Print Adjustment, so the fix is for the print alone and your edits stay right for the screen.
