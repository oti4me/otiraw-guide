OtiRAW User Guide

Layers

A composition is a stack of layers. Each layer is drawn over the ones below it. The Layers panel lists them, top first.

Kinds of layer

KindWhat it isHow to make one
PictureA photo or image. A photo from your library keeps its editAdd Picture, or the Picture tool
PaintBrush strokesPaint with the Brush on a new layer
FillA solid colour, a gradient or a patternNew Fill Layer
ShapeA rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star, line, arrow or pen pathA shape tool or the Pen
TextWordsThe Text tool
AdjustmentChanges the look of everything beneath itAdd Adjustment… (⌥⌘A)

New Layer is ⇧⌘N.

Each row shows the layer’s thumbnail, its kind, its name and an eye.

Opacity and blending

At the top of the Layers panel:

ControlWhat it does
OpacityHow see-through the layer is, effects included
Fill OpacityHow see-through its contents are, leaving its effects (shadow, stroke) at full strength
BlendHow the layer mixes with what is beneath it: Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Soft Light and the rest of the usual modes
Blend If…Shows the layer only where its own tones, or the tones beneath it, are within a range. Useful for blending skies and smoke without a mask

Clipping and nesting

A layer placed inside another is clipped to it: it shows only where its parent has pixels.

  • Clip to Layer Below (⌥⌘G) clips the chosen layer to the one under it.
  • Release from Parent (⌥⌘G again) undoes it.

The most common use is an adjustment that should change one layer only: clip the adjustment to that layer.

Move, size and turn

With the Move tool (V), drag a layer to move it, a corner to size it, and the handle above it to turn it. The bar shows X, Y, Width, Height and Rotation to set them exactly, and a Reference Point that says which point of the layer those numbers describe.

OptionWhat it does
SnapA moved layer snaps to the canvas’s middle and edges and to other layers. Hold ⌘ to move freely
Auto-SelectClicking the canvas chooses the layer under the pointer
ControlsShows the layer’s box and handles. Off, a drag only moves it
Distort, Skew, PerspectiveWhat the corner handles do
WarpShows the layer’s mesh: drag its points to bend it
Flip › Horizontal, VerticalMirrors the layer

Hold ⌘ with any other tool to get the Move tool until you let go.

Align and distribute

Choose two layers or more, then use Align Left, Center, Right, Top, Middle or Bottom, and Distribute Across or Down to space three or more evenly.

Working with several exposures or frames

Choose two layers or more:

CommandWhat it does
Auto-Align LayersLines the layers up on what they have in common
Stack: MedianKeeps what most frames agree on: removes people walking through a scene
Stack: MeanAverages the frames: reduces noise
Stack: FocusKeeps the sharpest part of each frame: focus stacking

Compound shapes

Choose two shapes or more and combine them under Geometry: join them into one, take the front ones out of the back one, keep only where they overlap, or keep only where they don’t. Each shape inside still moves and changes; Ungroup takes them apart.
