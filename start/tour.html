OtiRAW User Guide

A tour of the window

OtiRAW has one main window. Its parts are the same in every workspace, with different panels in them.

The pictures in this guide are drawn from the app’s interface with drawn scenes standing in for photographs.

The parts of the window

PartWhereWhat it holds
Workspace switcherLeft end of the bar above the filmstripButtons for Library, Edit, Compose and Print, then the previous and next photo arrows
Left panelsLeft sideIn the Library: Navigator, Library, Shoots, Sets, Folders, with Import… and Export… at the foot. In Edit: Navigator, Editing, Presets, Snapshots, History
CentreMiddleThe grid, the loupe, Compare, Survey, or the canvas
Right panelsRight sideIn the Library: Histogram, Quick Edit, Metadata, Assist, Keyword List. In Edit: the histogram, the six tool buttons and the tool’s sections
ToolbarUnder the centreView buttons, sort, flags, stars, labels, thumbnail size, zoom
Filter barThe same bar above the filmstrip (across the top in some layouts)The folder’s name and photo count, search, the filter’s flags, stars and labels, the Assist, Details and Keywords menus, and filter presets
FilmstripBottomThe folder’s photos in a row, in every workspace
StatusIn the bar above the filmstripProgress of thumbnails, previews, exports and sending

Show and hide the panels

ToPress
Show or hide the left panels⌘1
Show or hide the right panels⌘2
Show or hide the filmstrip⌘3
Show or hide all panels at onceTab
See the photo alone, full screenF

The same commands are in the View menu.

Each panel section has a header: click it to fold or unfold the section. Right-click a header for Expand All, Collapse All, Solo Mode (opening one section closes the others) and a list of sections to show or hide. In Edit you can also reorder the sections from that menu (Move Up, Move Down, Reset Section Order).

The toolbar

The toolbar under the grid or loupe holds the things you reach for most. Right-click it (or use its menu at the right end) to choose which groups it shows: View Modes, Sorting, Flagging, Rating, Color Label, Thumbnail Size, Zoom and Soft Proofing.

Layouts

OtiRAW comes with five layouts: arrangements of the panels with their own colour scheme. Choose one in Settings › Interface › Layout.

LayoutWhat it looks like
ClassicNavigator and folders on the left, histogram and metadata on the right, a toolbar under the grid and the filmstrip below, on black
StudioOne tool panel on the left with tabs (Sources, Inspect), the filter and search across the top, on neutral graphite
PalettesA command bar across the top, sources on the left, histogram and metadata palettes on the right, the image browser across the bottom, on blue-grey slate
Contact SheetThe grid first, one panel on the left with tabs, no filmstrip, on light greys with amber selections
MinimalOne quiet sidebar with small captions, no filmstrip, on midnight black

Each layout remembers its own colours. See Layouts, colours and controls.

Menus

Every command is in the menu bar, and the menus change with the workspace: Compose adds Image, Layer and Select menus, for example. Right-clicking a photo, a folder, a set, a layer or a panel opens a menu of what you can do with it.

Help while you work

  • Hover over a control to see what it does and its shortcut.
  • Help › Keyboard Shortcuts (⌘/) lists every shortcut; Edit Shortcuts… there opens Settings to change them.

  • Short messages appear briefly after an action (“Flagged as Pick”, “Exported 12 photos”), and anything that fails says why.
