OtiRAW User Guide

Layouts, colours and controls

OtiRAW’s look is yours to set: where the panels are, what colours they use, how sliders are drawn, how large everything is.

Layouts

A layout is an arrangement of the panels, with a colour scheme of its own. Choose one in Settings › Interface › Theme › Layout.

LayoutArrangementColours
ClassicNavigator and folders on the left; histogram, metadata and the folder’s settings on the right; a toolbar under the grid; the filmstrip below a breadcrumb barA black frame with bevelled edges
StudioOne tool panel on the left with tabs (Sources, Inspect), its tools under flat title bars; the filter and search across the top; the filmstrip belowNeutral graphite
PalettesA command bar with the filter and search across the top; sources on the left; histogram and metadata palettes on the right; the image browser across the bottom under its toolbarBlue-grey slate
Contact SheetThe grid first: one panel on the left with tabs for the sources and the photo’s information; no filmstripLight daylight greys with amber selections
MinimalOne quiet sidebar, with small captions instead of panel headers; no filmstripMidnight black

All five have the same features. Only where things are, and how they look, changes.

Colours

Colour scheme chooses the colours a layout uses: its own, or another layout’s.

Under it, Colours of … lists every colour of the scheme (panel backgrounds, text, the accent, selections). Click a colour to change it. Reset puts one colour back; Reset All puts the whole scheme back.

Each layout keeps its own scheme and colours, so switching layouts and back finds them as you left them.

For judging colour, a neutral, fairly dark interface is best: bright or coloured surroundings change how a photo looks.

Font

Settings › Interface › Text › Font sets the font of the whole interface: OtiRAW’s own, or any font installed on your computer. If a font is later removed, the interface goes back to its own.

Sliders

Settings › Interface › Controls:

SettingChoices
Slider styleRow: name at the left, value at the right, the most compact. Stacked: name above, the slider the full width of the panel, the value in a box with arrows to step it
Slider trackThin: a fine groove and a small knob. Medium: a slim bar in the slider’s colours with a larger knob. Thick: a rounded bar and a large knob

With Medium and Thick, a slider’s track shows what it does: dark to light for one that sets brightness, the colours themselves for temperature, tint and hue.

Accent colour: On checked boxes and main buttons fills ticked boxes and each panel’s main button (Export, Import, Apply) with the accent colour.

Compose’s icons

Settings › Interface › Compose sets the size of the icons in Compose’s bar, tools strip and layers bar (a right-click on each sets it too), and Plain icons draws them in one colour.
